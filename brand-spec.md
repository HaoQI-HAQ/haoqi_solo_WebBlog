# HAOQI / STUDIO design system

## Direction

An editorial portfolio for a game designer, visual designer, and AI composer. The site should feel like a moving cover for an independent creative practice: quiet enough to feel premium, but with small signals of an active system.

## Positioning

- Narrative role: hero cover -> practice proof -> selected archive -> capability matrix -> closing invitation.
- Viewing distance: desktop-first, read at roughly one metre, with a maximum content width of 1700px.
- Visual temperature: controlled, cool, and precise with one energetic accent.
- Capacity: large type and generous negative space carry the page; copy stays short until the visitor has chosen to explore.

## Tokens

- Ink: `#0d0f10`
- Paper: `#f1f0eb`
- Acid: `#d8ff4f`
- Fog: `#969d9e`
- Warm: `#e9d9ba`
- Spacing: 8px base unit, with section rhythm in 8px multiples.
- Radius: mostly sharp; 1px, 2px, 4px, with circles reserved for invitations and profile marks.
- Borders: hairline, low contrast. Shadows are rare and soft.

## Type

- Display: Space Grotesk, fallback Arial Narrow / Arial.
- Interface: IBM Plex Mono for labels, metadata, coordinates, and navigation.
- Headings use compressed tracking and low line-height; body copy stays between 13px and 18px.

## Motion

- Video background is atmospheric and low contrast, never the primary reading surface.
- Reveal-on-scroll uses a short vertical lift with a long, calm cubic-bezier curve.
- Hover states use color inversion, small rotations, or image scale rather than large shadows.
- Reduced-motion users receive a still poster and no continuous orbit motion.
